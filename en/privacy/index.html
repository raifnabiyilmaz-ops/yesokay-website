Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the YESOKAY app (the "app") and the website yesokay.app (the "website") handle information. It applies to everyone who uses the app or visits the website, wherever they live. Additional information for users in the EU/EEA and the UK and for users in Turkey is at the end.

1. Short summary

  • The app keeps your photos, settings and everything you do in it on your phone.
  • We do not run a server for the app. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us. If you send us an email yourself, or allow store diagnostics (anonymous crash reports from Apple or Google), that data may reach us.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not track you, in the app or on the website.
  • YESOKAY is an entertainment and memory app. It is not a medical, psychological or therapeutic tool.

2. Data controller

The data controller is Raif Nabi Yılmaz, Üsküdar/İstanbul 34700, Turkey. Email: help@yesokay.app.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean the data controller.

3. The app

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera only during the daily surprise photo moment (YES OKAY EYES), and only after you allow camera access. Your photos are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. We never receive them.

The app has no option to save your photos to your photo library or to share them, and it does not ask for access to your photo library.

Notifications

All notifications are local: your phone schedules and shows them. They are sent only if you allow notifications. You can turn each type off at any time in the app settings, and all of them in your phone settings. The app does not create or use push tokens, and no notification is sent from a server.

Trusted-time check

To know the real date and time (for example, so that the daily limits work fairly), the app sends a short HTTPS request to apple.com or google.com and reads only the "Date" header of the response. The request contains no personal content from the app. Like any internet request, it shows your IP address to Apple or Google, who handle it under their own privacy policies. We do not receive it.

Ratings

If the app asks whether you want to rate it, the rating window is provided by Apple or Google. Ratings and reviews are handled by them under their own terms and privacy policies.

Data on your device

Your presses, settings, language, photo moment times, the daily log and any notes you write stay on your device. We cannot access them. They are deleted when you delete the app.

If your phone backup (for example iCloud or Google backup) is turned on, your phone may include app data in that backup. This is between you and Apple or Google.

Links

If you tap a link in the app (for example to write to us, rate the app, or open the website), the related app or website opens and its own rules apply.

Version 1 of the app has no user account system.

4. App stores

You download the app from the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). Apple and Google process download, purchase and store data under their own terms and privacy policies. If you have allowed your device to share diagnostic data with app developers, Apple or Google may give us crash reports or app performance data that does not tell us who you are.

5. Website

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open any website, your browser automatically sends your IP address to the server. GitHub's servers record IP addresses automatically for their own security, under GitHub's own privacy statement and responsibility. We do not do this ourselves, we do not receive these records and we cannot see them. GitHub General Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

On our website we use no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. Fonts, images and code are served from the website itself, not from third-party services.

6. Emailing us

If you write to help@yesokay.app, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message.

  • We use your email only to reply to you.
  • We never add your email address to any list.
  • We never send you emails first: no newsletters, no marketing and no notifications by email.
  • Emails and the reply copy are deleted after replying, and no later than 30 days after receipt.
  • You can ask us to delete your email at any time.

Because there are no accounts, we cannot know whether you have deleted the app. We do not keep email addresses in any case.

Our email service is provided by Hostinger, which also manages our domain name.

7. No sale, no sharing, no advertising tracking

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with anyone for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

8. Children and age

The app is for people aged 18 or older. Users aged 15 to 17 may use the app only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for their use of the app. The app is not for children under 15. If we learn that a child under 15 has emailed us, we delete the email.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data, such as the right to access, correct or delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. You may also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. The sections for the EU/EEA and UK and for Turkey below give more detail.

Because the app keeps your data on your device, we usually hold no personal data about you. In most cases the only data we may hold is an email you sent us. We handle all requests by email. The fastest way to reach us, including for privacy and KVKK requests, is help@yesokay.app. We may ask for information to confirm that a request comes from you. We answer free of charge and within the time the law requires.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the little data we handle, for example by keeping app data on your device and keeping emails only as long as needed. No system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

Each version of this policy has a version number and an effective date. We will announce material changes in advance on the website and, where appropriate, in the app. We will not make material changes without notice.

12. Future features

If a future feature needs new processing of personal data, we will update this policy before the feature is released and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

13. Contact

Email: help@yesokay.app

14. Additional information for users in the EU/EEA and UK (GDPR)

This section applies if the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the UK GDPR applies to you.

Legal bases. We reply to your emails and handle your requests on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), and, for requests about your rights, to comply with our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR). We do not process the IP addresses of website visitors; GitHub records them under its own responsibility (see section 5).

Your rights. You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to data portability where it applies. If you gave consent (for example to notifications), you can withdraw it at any time in the app or phone settings.

Complaints. You can complain to the data protection supervisory authority of the country where you live or work, or where you think an infringement happened. In the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Transfers. We are based in Turkey. Hostinger, our email service provider, may store emails on servers outside the EU/EEA and the UK. GitHub acts under its own responsibility and its own privacy statement.

Retention. Emails and the reply copy are deleted after replying, and no later than 30 days after receipt. App data: only on your device, until you delete it or the app.

15. Additional information for users in Turkey (KVKK)

This section is our information notice under Article 10 of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK").

The Turkish version of this section is authoritative.

Data controller

Raif Nabi Yılmaz, Üsküdar/İstanbul 34700, Turkey. Email: help@yesokay.app.

Personal data processed

  • If you email us: your email address, your name if you include it, the content of your message and any attachments.

The data you create in the app (photos, settings, daily log, notes) is saved in the app on your phone. We cannot access it and we do not process it on our systems.

Website visits: GitHub, Inc., which hosts the website, automatically records visitors' IP addresses in its own systems, for its own security, under its own privacy statement and on its own responsibility. We do not keep these records, they are not passed to us and we cannot access them.

Purposes of processing

  • To reply to your emails and solve problems you report.
  • To receive and answer your requests, including requests under the KVKK.

Legal grounds (KVKK Article 5)

  • Article 5(2)(c): where your email concerns the use of the app under our Terms of Use, processing is necessary for the performance of that contract.
  • Article 5(2)(ç): to fulfil our legal obligations, for example when answering requests under Article 11.
  • Article 5(2)(f): our legitimate interest in answering people who contact us, provided that this does not harm your fundamental rights and freedoms.

Transfers

We do not transfer your personal data to anyone in Turkey, except to authorised public bodies when the law requires it. When you email us, your email may be stored on the servers of Hostinger, our domain name and email service provider, which may be located abroad. This transfer is occasional and not regular, and is made under Article 9(6) of the KVKK only to the extent necessary to answer your request to write to us.

Method of collection

Your data is collected by electronic means, when you send us an email.

Retention

Emails and the reply copy are deleted after replying, and no later than 30 days after receipt.

Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

You have the right to:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed;
  • request information if it has been processed;
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose;
  • know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred;
  • request correction if it is incomplete or incorrect;
  • request its deletion or destruction under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK;
  • request that the correction, deletion or destruction be notified to the third parties to whom it was transferred;
  • object to a result against you that arises only from automated analysis of your data;
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

How to apply

We handle all requests by email. The fastest way to reach us, including for privacy and KVKK requests, is help@yesokay.app. We answer free of charge as soon as possible and at the latest within 30 days, in line with the Communiqué on the Principles and Procedures for Applications to the Data Controller. If your application is rejected, the answer is insufficient or we do not answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board (Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulu).
